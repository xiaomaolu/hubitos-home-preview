# Commerce marks used in the local homepage preview

These marks identify marketplace examples. Their presence does not assert a
partnership, marketplace sales-data integration, or permission to publish.
Trademark rights and the applicable brand guidelines remain with each owner.

- Amazon and Shopee: Simple Icons 11.15.0, `icons/amazon.svg` and
  `icons/shopee.svg`, https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons.
  Simple Icons distributes its source under CC0, subject to its brand-specific
  disclaimer: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md.
  Shopee fill is set to orange; SVG geometry is preserved.
- TikTok Shop: existing client TikTok asset, with a small shopping-bag UI badge.
  This is not a separately sourced official TikTok Shop logo.
- SHEIN: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shein_Logo_2017.svg,
  original https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c5/Shein_Logo_2017.svg.
  The Commons description identifies SHEIN as the author and labels the simple
  text logo PD-textlogo, with trademark restrictions.
- Lazada: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lazada_(2019).svg,
  original https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/4d/Lazada_%282019%29.svg.
  The original SVG geometry is unchanged; only the viewport is cropped to the
  left-hand symbol, with corresponding intrinsic dimensions.
- Mercado Libre: https://github.com/pheralb/svgl/blob/main/static/library/mercado-libre.svg.
  The source project's MIT notice is reproduced below.
