Guide contents#

  • 01 Quick start: Enter Hubitos, learn the interface and complete your first task.
  • 02 Sign up & sign in: Register by email, sign in or reset a forgotten password.
  • 03 AI Assistant & image creation: Choose models, attach files, compare replies and generate images.
  • 04 Commerce images: Prepare products, image kits, detail pages, model images and reference-based edits.
  • 05 Commerce video: Prepare a product and reference, review the script, generate, edit and export a video.
  • 06 Connect social accounts: Connect a platform account, check plan limits and reconnect when needed.
  • 07 Create & optimize posts: Create several posts from a topic or improve an existing draft.
  • 08 Edit & publish posts: Review each platform version, attach media, save drafts and publish.
  • 09 Calendar & post management: Schedule posts, check time zones and resolve publishing failures.
  • 10 Social media analysis: Analyze public accounts or posts, read reports, export and save results.
  • 11 Trend Radar: Scan a topic, watch relevant trends and turn findings into content ideas.
  • 12 Interaction inbox: Sync interactions, filter them and review a suggested reply before sending.
  • 13 Post performance & interaction insights: Review post results and turn audience feedback into a content brief.
  • 14 Post-publishing automation: Set a one-time trigger and follow-up action for an X post.
  • 15 Operations tools: Use translation, guidelines, calendars, downloads and file-conversion tools.
  • 16 Library & files: Find and attach files, organize folders and restore saved versions.
  • 17 Settings, plans & credits: Manage language, desktop features, updates, plans and credits.
  • 18 Workflows & prompts: Follow complete workflows for everyday work, commerce and social content.
  • 19 Frequently asked questions: Find help with models, files, account connections, scheduling and generation.

Before you start#

  • The web and desktop apps share most features. Local file workspaces, system screenshots and system-wide text selection require the desktop app.
  • Available models, templates, permissions, quotas and prices are shown in the product.
